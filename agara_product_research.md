# Agara Matcha and Coffee Research

## Official Agara product pages

**Agara Coffee** (official page: https://agaraglobal.com/agara-coffee/) states that the product combines premium coffee with functional ingredients including Lion’s Mane, Chromemate® Chromium, Green Coffee Bean, Berberine HCL, Saffron, Lotus Leaf, Citrus Aurantium, and L-Theanine. Its stated benefit language includes clean sustained energy, sharper mental focus, daily wellness support, premium functional ingredients, and smooth rich coffee taste. The page includes an explicit disclaimer that the claims have not been evaluated or approved by the FDA and are not intended to diagnose, treat, mitigate, prevent, or cure disease.

**MATCHAGARA** (official page: https://agaraglobal.com/agara-matcha-matchagara/) describes a ceremonial-grade organic matcha combined with Lion’s Mane mushroom, L-Theanine, Berberine HCL, Chromemate® Chromium, Saffron, Lotus Leaf, and Citrus Aurantium. Its stated benefit language includes clean sustained energy, sharper focus, daily wellness support, premium matcha, and smooth rich taste. The page also uses stronger product-marketing terms such as metabolism support, appetite balance/control, mood balance, and “jitter-free” energy; these should be softened on the site unless supported by product-specific substantiation. The page includes the same FDA/general-wellbeing disclaimer.

## Research guardrails

- Treat the official Agara ingredient lists as brand-reported formula information, not independent verification of dosage, purity, or efficacy.
- Distinguish general evidence for matcha, caffeine, L-theanine, coffee, and other botanicals from evidence for the finished Agara products.
- Use “contains,” “is a source of,” and “may support” language rather than promising outcomes.
- Avoid disease-treatment, weight-loss, detox, blood-sugar-control, “jitter-free,” and guaranteed appetite/metabolism claims on the public landing page.
