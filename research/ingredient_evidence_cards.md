# Agara Ingredient Evidence Cards

> Quick-reference cards for each functional ingredient in the Agara product line.
> Evidence ratings: ★★★ = strong human clinical evidence · ★★ = moderate/promising · ★ = primarily preclinical

---

## Shared Stack (Both Products)

### Lion's Mane Mushroom ★★
- **Source:** *Hericium erinaceus* fruiting body/mycelium
- **Active compounds:** Hericenones, erinacines
- **Primary claim area:** Cognitive support, mental clarity
- **Evidence strength:** Promising in mild cognitive impairment; mixed in healthy adults
- **Landing page language:** "Contains Lion's Mane, traditionally valued for cognitive support"
- **Do not say:** "Boosts brain power" · "Enhances memory" · "Clinically proven"

### L-Theanine ★★★
- **Source:** Amino acid from *Camellia sinensis* (tea plant)
- **Primary claim area:** Calm focus, relaxed alertness
- **Evidence strength:** Strong when paired with caffeine — meta-analyses confirm attention benefits
- **Landing page language:** "L-Theanine and natural caffeine work together for calm, focused energy"
- **Do not say:** "Eliminates jitters" (claim needs product-specific substantiation)

### Berberine HCL ★★
- **Source:** Alkaloid from barberry, goldenseal, Oregon grape
- **Primary claim area:** Metabolic wellness
- **Evidence strength:** Good for glucose regulation in T2D; weak for weight loss
- **Landing page language:** "Contains Berberine HCL, which may support metabolic wellness"
- **Do not say:** "Controls blood sugar" · "Nature's Ozempic" · "Weight loss"

### ChromeMate® Chromium ★
- **Source:** Chromium polynicotinate (niacin-bound chromium)
- **Primary claim area:** Metabolic support
- **Evidence strength:** Inconclusive. Not proven for weight management or body composition.
- **Landing page language:** "Contains ChromeMate® chromium, a trace mineral involved in metabolism"
- **Do not say:** "Burns fat" · "Accelerates metabolism"

### Saffron ★★
- **Source:** *Crocus sativus* stigma extract
- **Primary claim area:** Mood balance, appetite support
- **Evidence strength:** Consistent for mild-moderate depression (30 mg/day). Satiereal® shown to reduce snacking.
- **Landing page language:** "Saffron extract is traditionally associated with mood balance"
- **Do not say:** "Treats depression" · "Appetite suppressant"

### Lotus Leaf ★
- **Source:** *Nelumbo nucifera* leaf extract
- **Active compound:** Nuciferine
- **Primary claim area:** Traditional wellness botanical
- **Evidence strength:** Animal studies only. No robust human trials.
- **Landing page language:** "Contains lotus leaf, a botanical traditionally used in wellness practices"
- **Do not say:** "Detoxifies" · "Burns fat" · "Cleanses"
- **⚠️ Safety note:** May inhibit CYP2D6 enzyme — potential drug interactions

### Citrus Aurantium ★
- **Source:** Bitter orange peel (*Citrus aurantium*) — active: p-synephrine
- **Primary claim area:** Energy, metabolic support
- **Evidence strength:** Insufficient and contradictory for weight loss. Blood pressure concerns.
- **Landing page language:** "Contains citrus aurantium, a traditional botanical"
- **Do not say:** "Thermogenic" · "Fat burner" · "Boosts metabolism"
- **⚠️ Safety note:** Structurally similar to ephedrine. May raise blood pressure with prolonged use.

## Agara Cafe Only

### Green Coffee Bean Extract ★★
- **Source:** Unroasted coffee beans — rich in chlorogenic acids (CGAs)
- **Primary claim area:** Antioxidant support
- **Evidence strength:** Promising for blood pressure; modest metabolic support. Early weight-loss hype based on retracted study.
- **Landing page language:** "Contains green coffee bean extract, a source of chlorogenic acid antioxidants"

### Guarana Seed ★★
- **Source:** *Paullinia cupana* — contains caffeine + theobromine
- **Primary claim area:** Energy, fatigue reduction
- **Evidence strength:** Well-established as caffeine source. Some evidence for sustained mental performance.
- **Landing page language:** "Contains guarana seed for sustained natural energy"

## MATCHAGARA Only

### Ceremonial-Grade Organic Matcha ★★★
- **Source:** Shade-grown, stone-ground *Camellia sinensis*
- **Key compounds:** EGCG (catechin), L-Theanine, caffeine
- **Primary claim area:** Antioxidant density, calm focus
- **Evidence strength:** Strong consensus on antioxidant content (2–3× steeped green tea). Strong for L-Theanine + caffeine cognitive synergy. Preliminary for metabolic markers.
- **Landing page language:** "Ceremonial-grade organic matcha — a concentrated source of antioxidants"
