## 3. Visual Identity System (extracted from assets)

### Color Palette

| Color | Hex (approx) | Usage |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| Agara Copper | `#B86538` | Brand mark, accents, CTAs, hover states |
| Living Green | `#9CAF72` | Matcha product, botanical accents |
| Warm Cream | `#F5F0E8` | Page backgrounds, packaging base |
| Dark Espresso | `#3D2B1F` | Coffee product, body text |
| Ivory White | `#FFFFFF` | Packaging face, clean space |
| Charcoal | `#2C2C2C` | Headlines, primary text |

### Typography

| Role | Typeface | Weight | Usage |
|------|----------|--------|-------|
| Headlines | Cormorant Garamond | 400/600 | "ELEVATE YOUR DAY", section titles |
| Body / UI | DM Sans | 400/500/700 | Paragraphs, buttons, navigation |
| Packaging | Custom spaced uppercase | — | Product names, benefit labels |

### Iconography
- 5-icon benefit system: circle-outlined, thin stroke, copper on packaging
- Three macro pillars (web): Energy ⚡ · Focus ◎ · Balance 🌿
- Style: Minimalist line icons with copper fill

### Photography Direction
- **Lighting:** Natural, warm — window-light style. No studio flash or neon.
- **Surfaces:** Raw wood, stone, linen — organic textures
- **Props:** Bamboo whisk, ceramic cups, whole beans, loose matcha powder, tea leaves
- **Mood:** Calm abundance. Intentional, unhurried. "Morning ritual" energy.
- **Color grading:** Warm midtones, creamy highlights, muted shadows
