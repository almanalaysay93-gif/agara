# Agara Voice Rails

**Voice in one sentence:** We talk like a well-read herbalist with a trust fund—warm and grounded, never overselling, always honest about what we know and don't.

---

## Voice Rails

### 1. Botanical-Editorial (not Marketing Hype)
**Do:** "Lion's Mane contains hericenones, compounds under study for cognitive wellness."
**Don't:** Overstated brain or memory claims; avoid guaranteed outcomes (see banned words list).

### 2. Warm Restraint (not False Certainty)
**Do:** "Research suggests L-Theanine paired with caffeine may support calm focus."
**Don't:** Guaranteed focus claims or unsubstantiated "smooth energy" language (see banned words list).

### 3. Honest About Limits (not Defensive)
**Do:** "Chromium plays a role in metabolism, though evidence for weight effects remains inconclusive."
**Don't:** Avoiding chromium entirely or overselling it as proven.

### 4. Sourced, Not Slurred (Proof Points Over Adjectives)
**Do:** "Ceremonial matcha contains 2–3x the antioxidants of steeped tea."
**Don't:** "Stunning superfood" or "revolutionary wellness."

### 5. Ritualistic, Not Clinical (Speaking to Daily Life)
**Do:** "A moment to steady yourself before the day demands it."
**Don't:** "Optimized biohacking stack" or "pharmaceutical-grade formulation."

---

## Banned Words (Hard Veto)

The following words and phrases are forbidden in all site copy:

- jitter-free / jitter free
- burns fat / fat burner
- thermogenic
- detox / cleanse
- clinically proven
- controls blood sugar
- Ozempic / metformin
- appetite suppressant
- weight loss
- boosts metabolism / accelerates metabolism
- boosts brain power / enhances memory
- cures / treats depression

---

## Approved Language Patterns

- "Contains [ingredient], traditionally valued for…"
- "May support [function]"
- "Is a source of [compound]"
- "Research suggests…"
- "Evidence is mixed / limited / promising…"
- "Traditionally associated with…"
- "[Ingredient] is the subject of ongoing research into…"

## Proof Points Inventory

### Product Differentiation
- **Dual-product line** (coffee + matcha) — most competitors lead with one
- **Botanical stack beyond mushrooms** — berberine, saffron, chromium, lotus leaf rarely found in functional coffee/matcha
- **Community ambassador model** — lifestyle brand positioning vs. pure supplement DTC
- **Ethical sourcing** — small-batch roasted South American Arabica, non-GMO

### Ingredient Evidence (What We Can Actually Claim)

**Strong claims (★★★ evidence):**
- L-Theanine + caffeine: Meta-analyses confirm attention, reaction time, working memory benefits
- Ceremonial matcha: Contains 2–3x antioxidants of steeped tea (EGCG catechins) + L-Theanine + caffeine synergy

**Moderate claims (★★ evidence):**
- Saffron: Consistent research for mild-to-moderate mood support (30 mg/day); Satiereal® extract shown to reduce snacking frequency
- Berberine: Good evidence for metabolic support; weight-loss claims remain inconsistent
- Green coffee bean extract: Promising for antioxidants; weight-loss hype was based on retracted study
- Guarana: Established as sustained caffeine source; some evidence for mental performance over time
- Lion's Mane: Promising in mild cognitive impairment; mixed in healthy adults

**Weak claims (★ evidence):**
- Chromium: Essential trace mineral for metabolism; clinical weight/composition effects unproven
- Lotus leaf: Traditional use documented; human clinical trials limited; animal studies only
- Citrus aurantium: Evidence insufficient and contradictory; blood pressure concerns documented

### What We Cannot Say
- No metabolic outcome claims without robust human evidence (see banned words)
- No pharmaceutical drug comparisons (see banned words)
- No purification or cleansing language (see banned words)
- No guaranteed outcomes
- No "smooth energy" or stimulant-free claims without product-specific substantiation
- No disease-treatment language
